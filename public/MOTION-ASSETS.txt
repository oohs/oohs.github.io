Motion mask notes
=================

oriental-loong-mask.avif
  Original raster artwork prepared for this personal-site prototype. It is
  never displayed directly; the animation samples its dark ink as attractor
  coordinates for letter fragments and particles.

cursive-long-mask.png
  A cleaned sampling crop of Huai Su's cursive rendering of 龍 from
  "Compilation of Cursive Characters" (草字彙), authored by Shi Liang during
  the Qing dynasty. The source scan contains eight historic renditions and is
  marked PD-Art on Wikimedia Commons.

  Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cursive_characters_dragon.jpg

  The site uses only the high-contrast coordinates of the selected glyph as a
  particle attractor; the scan itself is not shown.
